# Brief: Overview

**This brief covers only the Overview section of the Navilott report:
why the report exists, who it is for, who built what, and how the work
was done. It does not explain how the robot works; later sections do.**

## The section's main idea

The report is a self-evaluation of one part of a three-person senior design
project: the vision stack of Navilott, a small self-driving robot. It is
written by the engineer who built most of that stack. Its job is to show
what was designed, what evidence exists that it works, and what is still
unproven, honestly enough that a reader can trust the parts it claims.
